Open firmware for the SMK-37

The 37-key controller with pads, knobs, faders and wheels, turned into a synth, a groovebox, a drone machine or a chord harp: 25 open-source firmwares, made for it or ported from the FM-1, each installed from its maker's installer. Chrome or Edge.

Not connected
☕ Buy me a coffee
Pick your keyboard's own model in the installer. Most firmwares here come as three packages: SMK-37 Pro, SMK-37 Elite and MKE-P37 (a Starrykey 37 Play takes the Pro's). The FM-1 ports are the firmwares of the FM-1 hub, built for the SMK-37 by genkigenki with fm1-to-smk37. Everything installs in Chrome or Edge over USB from its maker's installer. Back up your sounds first; M-Vave's updater takes the keyboard back to its factory firmware, and holding OCT− while switching it on is the rescue.

Felucca SMK0.9.27

by Chance Roth (ChanceTheMaker)
synthgrooveboxPro · Elite

Felucca's synthesis and sequencing on the SMK's 37 keys, pads, knobs, faders, wheels and display: engines from FM6 to phase distortion, chiptune and samples, a step sequencer, a browser Studio and a simulator.

Its installer builds the package from the keyboard's official firmware 016 and its calibration: 016 first, back up before.

OberVave1.3.1 beta

An 8-voice analog-style poly after the classic American polys of 1979-84, on four tracks: each its own sound, arp and sequence, a mixer with chorus, delay, plate reverb and drive sends, a master with DJ filter and compressor; the step sequencer on the 16 pads, filter and effects on the fader banks.
