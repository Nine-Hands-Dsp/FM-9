The Firmware Hub · FM-1 Jukebox

Share your FM-1 tunes

Tracks with an FM-1 in them – alone or with the rest of the studio – on two decks right in your browser – mix them or just let them play. Made one yourself? Upload it: it goes into the Jukebox with your name on the display and your link in the credits.

⬆ Upload your track

max. 1 track a day

Power on – the FM-1 Tunes are in the list. K1 / K2 play deck A / B, K3 / K4 load a track onto it, A / B the crossfader. VIBE DJ, the same firmware as on the VIBE FM.

⬆ Upload your track

Send a track you made with an FM-1 in it. It doesn't have to be FM-1 only – your other synths, drums, vocals, your DAW: all welcome, the FM-1 just has to be part of it. I'm putting them into the Jukebox, with your name on the display and your link in the credits. :)

– zVen

  1. MP3 or WAV, up to 30 MB – one track a day.
  2. Only your own music: no samples of other people's records, no track you signed away (collecting society, label).
  3. Legit? Then it goes in – we only check that it is yours and an FM-1 is in it.
  4. Want it out again? One mail and it is gone.

Sign in with your hub account – no password: we mail you a 6-digit code. New here? The same step makes your account.

Your address is only used to sign you in (and for what you tick later in your account): no sharing. Privacy
